\section{Layer-0 Safety-Validation Notes}
\label{app:perhead}

The paper uses one additional artifact to validate all-head execution and certificate bookkeeping without presenting it as a full-model benchmark. That artifact executes only layer \(0\), then applies the final RMSNorm and language-model head. Its logits, negative log-likelihood, and perplexity are therefore diagnostic pseudo-logit quantities rather than full \modeldisplay{} quality measurements.

This artifact is valuable for three narrower questions: whether all 32 query heads and all 8 KV heads are exercised correctly, whether the repaired sequential execution remains memory-safe, and whether the storage-accounting categories remain disjoint. It is not used as evidence for full-model language quality.
