\section{AI-Assistance Disclosure}
\label{app:ai}

AI tools assisted with:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
  \item code drafting and repair;
  \item experiment orchestration and packaging;
  \item bibliography organization;
  \item LaTeX drafting and figure generation;
  \item evidence consistency auditing.
\end{enumerate}

The student remains responsible for:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
  \item the research question and hypothesis;
  \item understanding the theorem statements and proof logic;
  \item experimental design and interpretation;
  \item all final scientific claims, caveats, and conclusions;
  \item oral defense and mentor-facing explanation.
\end{enumerate}

This disclosure does not claim that the student has personally read every cited paper in full; the frozen corpus metadata currently records student read status as pending unless independently documented otherwise.
