\section{Reproducibility Checklist}
\label{app:repro}

The paper is designed to be reproducible from local evidence without rerunning model inference during manuscript compilation. Verified artifacts are first converted into compact CSV and JSON summaries by \artifactpath{scripts/extract_verified_data.py}; the manuscript then reads only those derived files.

The current paper package intentionally omits model weights, tensor caches, and full experiment checkpoints. It includes only the compact evidence required to rebuild the manuscript, audit the reported numbers, and update the paper once a verified full-model quality package becomes available.
